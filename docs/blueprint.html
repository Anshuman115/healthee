1 · Product thesis

The honesty contract

Healthee is not a wellness app that congratulates you. It is a companion that tells the truth about your body, grounded in graded research, and nudges you toward the next achievable improvement. Every architectural decision below serves five principles — most already live in the codebase in some form; the plan makes them systemic rather than incidental.

Never lies, never flatters.

Every interpretive sentence carries a citation into the graded research corpus or it doesn't ship — the existing validator contract (llm/validator.py, cite-or-refuse) becomes a platform guarantee enforced at one choke point, not per-endpoint.

Confidence is part of the answer.

Every number the user sees carries its data quality: how many days fed it, whether a feed is stale, whether the value is measured, derived, or provisional. "Not enough data" is always preferred over an optimistic guess. The Data-check banner grows into a per-metric confidence system.

Nudge, don't please.

The recovery score, challenges with frozen outcome ledgers, and adaptive difficulty already measure real change instead of streak theater. The weekly review closes the loop: what actually improved, what didn't, and the one thing to focus on next — even when the news is bad.

Science is a pipeline, not a vibe.

The 55-note research corpus with evidence grades (2/3) and primary-source verification is the product's spine. Derivations (Phillips SRI, Tanaka HRmax, Banister TRIMP, Gompertz bio-age…) stay documented, tested, and traceable to their notes.

The user owns the data.

Self-hosted backend, and after this plan: 60 days of history living on the device itself, offline analytics, off-box backups, and CSV/PDF export. The companion works on an airplane and survives a dead VPS.

3 · End-to-end architecture

One system, three tiers

The strap is the sensor. The phone is the collector and a first-class analytical node with 60 days of memory. The server is the canonical brain — full history, the science layer, and the grounded intelligence. Everything the user sees works offline; everything interpretive is grounded in research.

The flow, end to end

  • Collect. The BLE kit authenticates (ECDH sect163k1), pages every fetch type with keep-on-device ACKs and gap-skip, parses with version-guarded layouts, and lands everything in the local store with per-metric watermarks. Nothing is destructive; partial syncs always resume.
  • Render instantly. The UI never waits on the network. Device analytics computes provisional baselines, trends, and a provisional recovery score straight from the local store — tagged provisional until the server confirms.
  • Push up, sync down. The sync engine pushes new data to /ingest/helio (idempotent), then pulls /api/sync/down?since= to mirror server-derived dailies back into the local store. Server values replace provisional ones; disagreements beyond tolerance are logged — the honesty contract applies internally too.
  • Derive & analyze. The server derives the full science layer per day/night, runs the analytics chain (correlate → recs → insight) as supervised jobs whose failures page you on Telegram instead of vanishing.
  • Ground & speak. Every LLM surface — recs, insights, coach, weekly review — passes through one grounded-ask choke point: research citations validated, refusal domains enforced, confidence attached. The coach acts only through typed tools and never claims an action it didn't perform.
  • Survive. Nightly dumps off-box; the device itself holds 60 days; a dead VPS means degraded (no LLM, no full history), not dead.

4 · Backend modularization

From one file to domain packages

The v2 core (derive.py, vo2max_submax.py, dem.py, ingest.py) is already clean — it moves, it doesn't change. The work is dismantling api/app.py (4,463 lines) into routers that own nothing but HTTP, and giving every module explicit, one-directional dependencies.

src/healthee/
  core/        config · db (one psycopg_pool) · auth (bearer dep) · notify (telegram)
  ingest/      helio payload validation · upserts · daily-total overrides   ← v2/ingest.py
  derive/      science layer, unchanged                                     ← v2/derive.py, vo2max_submax.py, dem.py
  analytics/   baselines · correlations · anomalies · cutoffs · bio-age     ← v2-native queries (seam fixed)
  insights/    llm client · grounded ask · validator · context · recs
               challenges · programs · coach tools                         ← llm/*, no upward imports
  research/    corpus loader (mtime-cached) · grading · note export
  api/         app.py (wiring only, <150 lines) + routers/
                 ingest · today · sleep · activity · workouts · recovery
                 insightsx · coach · challenges · programs · logs · gps
                 profile · syncdown
  jobs/        scheduler · event chain with per-step capture → notify       ← replaces Popen(shell=True)
  db/          schema_v2 · migrations (numbered) · backup entrypoint

Rules that keep it modular

  • Dependency direction: api → insights/analytics → derive/ingest → core. Nothing imports upward (kills recs.py:393 importing the API app, and the ask.py:75 reflection hack).
  • One connection pool in core/db.py, one transaction convention (context-managed, no autocommit mix). Kills the connection-per-metric pattern in context building.
  • One grounded-ask choke point in insights/: prompt → LLM → citation validation → confidence tagging. Every surface calls it; no endpoint talks to the LLM client directly.
  • The event chain becomes a job: in-process run_chain(day) with per-step try/except → Telegram on failure, replacing Popen("healthee correlate && …", shell=True).
  • v2-native reads: analytics and insights query sample/derived_daily/sleep_session directly. The v1-compat views (source of the five live bugs) are frozen in Phase 0 and deleted at the end of Phase 1.
  • Delete on sight: v2/api.py (self-documented dead), v1 ingest endpoints (would crash on the non-updatable view), the JSON-file profile, db/init.py+v1 schema paths.
  • Migration safety net: before the split, snapshot every endpoint's JSON for a fixed test day; after each router extraction, responses must be byte-identical. The split is pure motion, zero behavior.

6 · On-device data tier

Sixty days in your pocket

The SQLite store graduates from sync buffer to first-class data tier. The app renders everything from local data and treats the network as an enhancer, not a dependency.

TableRetentionContents & size
sample60 daysPer-minute metrics (hr, steps, stress, spo2, hrv, temp, resp) — ~500k rows ≈ 15–25 MB. Pruned after each successful push; watermarks unaffected (MAX ts).
sleep_session400 daysNights + naps with stage timelines — tiny, keep long for regularity analytics.
workout400 daysSummaries + local GPS tracks for recorded routes.
daily_metric new400 daysMirror of server derived_daily (~40 metrics/day ≈ 16k rows/year — negligible). Each row tagged origin: server | provisional.
meta—Watermarks, sync health, backfill guards, last reconcile.

Sync-down & reconciliation

  • New endpoint GET /api/sync/down?since=YYYY-MM-DD returns derived_daily rows (+ flags, + sleep-session summaries) since the device's last reconcile. Called after every successful push; a full 400-day pull on fresh install restores history to a new phone in one request.
  • Server wins. Provisional device values are replaced by server values on reconcile. A disagreement beyond tolerance (e.g. recovery ±5 pts) is recorded in sync health — drift between the two engines is a bug and gets surfaced, never hidden.
  • Offline behavior: no network → the entire app renders from daily_metric + local samples, with a quiet offline · provisional tag on derived values. LLM surfaces show their last cached text with its date, clearly stamped — a stale insight presented as fresh would violate the contract.
  • Tertiary backup for free: with 60 days of raw samples + 400 days of dailies on-device, a dead VPS loses no recent data — re-ingest from the device rebuilds the canonical store (push is already idempotent).

7 · On-device analytics

A small, honest engine in Dart

Not a port of the whole science layer — a deliberate subset that makes the device self-sufficient for descriptive analytics, while inference (correlations, cutoffs, bio-age, LLM) stays server-side where full history lives.

ModuleComputesMirrors
baselines28-day median ± MAD per metric, personal bandsanalytics/baselines.py
trends7 vs 28-day deltas, direction + magnitude for sparkline cardsserver trend logic
anomaliesrobust z ≥ 2 flags on today's valuesanalytics/anomalies.py
recoveryprovisional recovery score from local HRV/RHR/sleep/load inputsderive.py recovery
sleepdebt vs need, 14-day SRI, tonight's lever inputssleep-health derive
confidenceper-metric data coverage: feed freshness, days-of-data, gapsData-check logic, deepened
  • Pure functions, shared fixtures. Every module is side-effect-free Dart. The Python engine exports golden fixtures (input day-window → expected outputs); the Dart suite must match within tolerance. Parity is CI-enforced — two engines, one truth.
  • Wake-up moment: strap syncs at 6:30, recovery score and tonight's sleep lever are on screen before the server round-trip finishes — then quietly confirmed.
  • Grounding travels: a generated research_summaries.json (note id, claim, grade) ships in app assets, so even offline cards can cite — e.g. “SRI ≥ 70 associates with lower mortality [sleep_regularity_index]” — from the same corpus the server cites.

8 · Companion intelligence

Making honesty a feature, not a tone

  • Confidence on every card. Each metric card exposes its evidence: days of data, feed freshness, measured/derived/provisional origin, evidence grade of the interpretation. Low confidence changes the copy — “HRV trend unclear: only 4 nights of data” — instead of hiding.
  • The weekly review. Sunday digest, in-app + Telegram: the week against your own baselines, challenge outcomes from the frozen ledger, what measurably improved / regressed / stayed flat, and one focus for next week. Generated through grounded-ask; unflinching by design — a bad week is reported as a bad week, with the mechanism and the smallest next step.
  • Coach with a memory. The outcome ledger, adherence rates, and past week-over-week deltas enter coach context — “last time we raised MVPA 20% your HRV followed in 10 days” is measured personal evidence, the strongest nudge there is. The anti-hallucination guard (never claim an unexecuted action) stays absolute.
  • Graduated plans. The sleep “Tonight” lever grows into the multi-week ladder (already roadmapped) using the programs machinery; the VO₂max plan keeps its projected-trajectory honesty — projections labeled as projections.
  • Export. CSV per metric + PDF summary. A companion that hides your data from you isn't yours.
  • Recovery-weight tuning after ~90 days of real use: validate score weights against your own outcome data (did high-recovery days actually precede better sessions?) — self-honesty about our own model.
